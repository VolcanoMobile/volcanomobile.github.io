# Moodtune — Privacy Policy

Last updated: Oct 12, 2026 · @Philippe

## Overview

Moodtune does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. The app makes songs entirely on your phone and has no access to the internet.

This policy covers the Moodtune Android app (package `net.volcanomobile.pocketstudio`), published by Volcano Mobile ("we").

## Data we collect

We collect no data. Moodtune has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.

The text you type to describe a song is read only by the AI model built into the app, which runs on your phone's processor. That text, and the song made from it, never leave your device through Moodtune.

## Permissions

Moodtune does not request internet access, location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions.

| Permission | Why Moodtune uses it |
| --- | --- |
| Notifications | Shows the progress of a song being made, with a button to cancel it. |
| Foreground service (media processing) | Keeps making a song you started while you use other apps. |
| Wake lock | Keeps the processor awake until the song you started is finished. |

## Your songs and exports

Saved songs, and the text you typed for them, are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read them.

When you export a song as MIDI or M4A, Android's file picker asks you where to save it. Moodtune writes only to the place you choose. What happens to the file after that is up to you and the app or service you choose.

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include Moodtune's saved songs in your device backup to your Google account. Google handles that backup under its own privacy policy; we never receive it.

## Third parties

Moodtune shares no data with third parties. Its open-source building blocks (ONNX Runtime for the AI model, FluidSynth and Oboe for sound) run inside the app on your phone. ONNX Runtime's optional telemetry is switched off and removed, and the app has no internet permission, so nothing can be sent.

## Children, security and deletion

- **Children:** Moodtune collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.
- **Security:** your songs stay in app-private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox and your device lock.
- **Deletion:** delete a song in the app to remove it. Uninstalling Moodtune, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes all its data. Files you exported stay where you saved them until you delete them.

## Changes and contact

If Moodtune ever starts collecting data, we will update this policy and the date at the top before that version is released.
